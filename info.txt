npm install -g autocannon
autocannon -c 100 -d 30 http://localhost:3000/api/crud/10

For example:
autocannon -c 10 -d 20 http://localhost:3000/api/crud/10
then:
autocannon -c 100 -d 20 http://localhost:3000/api/crud/10
then:
autocannon -c 500 -d 20 http://localhost:3000/api/crud/10
Compare the results.
